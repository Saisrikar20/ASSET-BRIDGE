# Bank deposits (Reserve Bank of India Statutory Framework)

## Primary Regulatory Master Directions
* **Statutory Basis**: Sections 45ZA to 45ZG of the Banking Regulation Act, 1949.
* **Rules Framework**: Banking Companies (Nomination) Rules, 1985 / 2025.
* **Master Direction**: *Reserve Bank of India (Commercial Banks – Responsible Business Conduct) Directions, 2025* (Updated as on October 1, 2026), Notification No. `RBI/DOR/2025-26/170`, Circular No. `DOR.MCS.REC.No.89/01-01-032/2025-26`, Section E (Paras 281 to 322).
* **Supervisory Directions**: *Reserve Bank of India (Commercial Banks – Miscellaneous) Supervisory Directions, 2026*, Notification No. `RBI/DoS/2026-27/416`, Chapter VI (Inoperative Accounts and Unclaimed Deposits).

---

## 1. Statutory Nomination Facility & Official Forms
Nomination in bank deposit accounts is governed by Section 45ZA of the Banking Regulation Act:
* **Form DA-1**: Statutory form to make a nomination in respect of bank deposits (Rule 2(1)).
* **Form DA-2**: Statutory form to cancel an existing nomination (Rule 2(5)).
* **Form DA-3**: Statutory form to vary or change a nomination (Rule 2(6)).

---

## 2. Claiming as a Registered Nominee (Annex V)
* **Standard Form**: Claimants file **Annex V (Claim Form for Accounts with Nominee / Survivorship Clause)**.
* **Valid Legal Discharge**: Under Section 45ZA(2), payment by the bank to the registered nominee constitutes a **full, complete, and valid legal discharge** of the bank's liability.
* **Nominee as Trustee**: Under Supreme Court jurisprudence (*Sarbati Devi v. Usha Devi*), the nominee receives deposit funds as a legal trustee for the rightful legal heirs under applicable succession law (Hindu Succession Act, Indian Succession Act). Nomination does not override testamentary succession or a valid Will.
* **Strict Prohibition on Demanding Legal Representation (Para 286)**: Banks are **strictly prohibited** from insisting on a Succession Certificate, Letter of Administration, Probate of Will, or obtaining a Bond of Indemnity / Surety from the registered nominee, regardless of the deposit amount.
* **Required Documents from Nominee**:
  1. Claim Form (Annex V).
  2. Original / certified Death Certificate issued under the Civil Registration System (CRS).
  3. Officially Valid Document (OVD) for KYC verification (PAN, Aadhaar, Passport).

---

## 3. Claiming without a Nominee / Intestate Claims (Annex VI)
When the account holder dies without nomination, or when the nominee has predeceased the holder, all surviving legal heirs claim jointly:

### Simplified Procedure up to ₹15,00,000 Threshold (Para 288)
* RBI mandates a simplified settlement threshold limit of **₹15,00,000 (Fifteen Lakh Rupees)** aggregate balance (or a higher limit established by the bank's Board).
* Up to ₹15 Lakhs, banks **shall not demand a court Succession Certificate or Probate of Will**.
* **Required Documentation for Simplified Settlement**:
  1. Claim Form for Non-Nominated Accounts (**Annex VI**).
  2. Certified Death Certificate.
  3. Proof of Legal Heirship (Tahsildar Legal Heir Certificate, Revenue Court Certificate, or notarized **Annex IX Heir Affidavit**).
  4. Letter of Disclaimer / No Objection Certificate (**Annex VIII**) signed by non-claiming legal heirs relinquishing rights in favour of the applicant heir.
  5. Letter of Indemnity and Surety (**Annex VII**) on non-judicial stamp paper per State Stamp Act.

### Claims Exceeding ₹15,00,000
For intestate claims exceeding ₹15 Lakhs where there is dispute or lack of consensus among heirs, banks may require a formal **Succession Certificate** issued by a competent Civil District Court under Part X of the Indian Succession Act, 1925.

---

## 4. Mandatory Turnaround Time (TAT) and Delay Penalty (Paras 319-322)
* **15-Day Statutory Deadline**: Banks must settle and disburse the claim within **15 calendar days** from the date of receipt of all required claim documents.
* **Statutory Delay Penalty for Deposits**: If a bank delays payment beyond 15 calendar days due to its own fault or administrative latency, the bank must pay interest at **Bank Rate + 4.00% p.a.** (currently **9.75% p.a.** based on prevailing 5.75% Bank Rate) on the entire claim amount from the date of document submission until final disbursement.
* **Statutory Delay Penalty for Lockers**: Fixed statutory compensation of **₹5,000 per day** of delay beyond 15 calendar days in arranging joint inventory release.

---

## 5. Joint Accounts with Survivorship Mandate
* For joint accounts with "Either or Survivor" or "Former or Survivor" mandate, the surviving holder is entitled to receive the balance on producing the death certificate of the deceased joint holder.
* No legal heir certificate or indemnity is required for survivor payout.

---

## 6. Multi-Branch & Digital Claim Submission Rights (Paras 316-318)
* Claimants have the statutory right to lodge deceased depositor claim applications at **any branch of the bank** across India, without being forced to travel to the home branch where the account was opened.
* Banks must offer online claim lodgement, status tracking, and provide formal SMS/email acknowledgment with a unique tracking reference within 3 working days.

---

## 7. Inoperative Accounts, Unclaimed Deposits & the DEA Fund
* **Inoperative Status**: Savings or current accounts with no customer-induced transaction for **2 years** are classified as inoperative.
* **Zero Charges for Reactivation**: Banks cannot charge any fee for reactivating an inoperative account. Re-activation can be completed at any branch or via Video-KYC (V-CIP).
* **DEA Fund Transfer**: Balances in inoperative accounts or unclaimed matured deposits for **10 years or more** are transferred to the RBI Depositor Education and Awareness (DEA) Fund.
* **Full Reclaim Right**: Transferred funds remain 100% claimable by depositors or legal heirs with statutory interest through the bank, which processes the claim and settles from the DEA Fund.
* **RBI UDGAM Portal**: The centralized UDGAM portal (Unclaimed Deposits – Gateway to Access inforMation) allows heirs to search across 30+ participating banks using PAN, Voter ID, Passport, or Date of Birth.
