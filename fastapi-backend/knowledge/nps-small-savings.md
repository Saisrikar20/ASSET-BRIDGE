# NPS and post office small savings
## NPS on death of the subscriber
When a National Pension System subscriber dies before retirement, the entire accumulated pension wealth is paid to the nominee or legal heirs; there is no requirement to buy an annuity. The claimant submits the withdrawal form for death, the death certificate, PRAN details, KYC and bank proof through the Point of Presence (bank) or the Central Recordkeeping Agency (Protean or KFintech). Without a nomination, legal heir documents are required.
## PPF account on death
A Public Provident Fund account cannot be continued by the nominee; it is closed on the holder's death and the balance paid out, even before the 15-year maturity. The nominee or legal heir submits the post office death claim form, death certificate, passbook and KYC at the account office. Up to a limit set by India Post, legal heirs without a nomination can be paid on an affidavit and indemnity instead of a succession certificate.
## NSC, KVP, SCSS and other certificates
National Savings Certificates, Kisan Vikas Patra, Senior Citizens' Savings Scheme and post office time deposits follow the same death-claim process at the post office. Matured certificates that were never encashed keep their value and can still be claimed by heirs.
