# Legal heir and succession certificates by state
## Legal heir certificate vs succession certificate
A legal heir certificate is an administrative document issued by a revenue official (Tahsildar, SDM, Village Officer) that lists the surviving family members of a deceased person. A succession certificate is issued by a civil court under Section 372 of the Indian Succession Act, 1925 and authorises the holder to collect the deceased's debts and securities, such as bank deposits, shares and other financial assets. Many institutions accept a legal heir certificate for smaller amounts and insist on a succession certificate for larger ones. A succession certificate takes longer — often several months — because the court issues public notice and invites objections.
## Tamil Nadu
Legal Heir Certificate is issued by the Tahsildar. Apply online through TN e-Sevai or at an e-Sevai centre with the death certificate, ID and address proof of all heirs, and a self-declaration. A Village Administrative Officer (VAO) and Revenue Inspector enquiry precedes issue, typically within about 15 to 30 days.
## Kerala
A Legal Heirship Certificate is issued by the Village Officer or Tahsildar, applied for through the e-District portal or Akshaya centres.
## Karnataka
A Family Tree certificate or Survival certificate is issued by the Tahsildar, applied for at the Nadakacheri (Atalji Janasnehi Kendra) or online.
## Andhra Pradesh and Telangana
A Family Member Certificate is issued by the Tahsildar and applied for through MeeSeva centres.
## Maharashtra
Financial institutions usually ask for a succession certificate or heirship certificate from the civil court; a legal heir certificate from the Tahsildar is less commonly used for financial claims.
## Delhi
A Legal Heir Certificate is issued by the SDM or Revenue Department and applied for on the e-District Delhi portal.
## Uttar Pradesh
A Varisan Praman Patra (heirship certificate) is issued through the tehsil, applied for on the e-District UP portal.
## West Bengal
Institutions typically require a succession certificate issued by the district court.
## Gujarat
An heirship certificate is issued by the Mamlatdar or by civil court, depending on the purpose and the amount involved.
## Common documents
Almost every state asks for: the death certificate, identity and address proof of every legal heir, proof of relationship, an affidavit or self-declaration listing all heirs, and, in some states, a no-objection statement from the other heirs.
