# Mutual funds and shares (SEBI)
## Mutual fund transmission
On the death of a unitholder, the nominee or legal heir submits a transmission request form to the registrar and transfer agent (CAMS or KFintech) or the AMC. Documents: death certificate, claimant KYC, bank proof, and — when there is no nominee — legal heir documents and an indemnity. A single request covers all folios serviced by the same RTA. Units are transferred to the claimant's own folio and can then be redeemed.
## Tracing forgotten folios
MF Central (run jointly by CAMS and KFintech) gives a consolidated view of folios by PAN. SEBI's MITRA platform (Mutual Fund Investment Tracing and Retrieval Assistant) helps investors and heirs find inactive or unclaimed folios that may not be linked to a PAN.
## Demat holdings
Shares in a demat account are transmitted through the Depository Participant (via NSDL or CDSL). The claimant needs a demat account of their own, the transmission request form, death certificate and, if there is no nominee, legal heir or succession documents with an indemnity. SEBI has standardised and simplified the transmission documentation across DPs and RTAs.
## IEPF – unpaid dividends and shares
Dividends unpaid or unclaimed for seven consecutive years, and the underlying shares, are transferred by companies to the Investor Education and Protection Fund (IEPF). Heirs can reclaim them by filing Form IEPF-5 online and sending the verified documents to the company's nodal officer.
## Nomination requirement
SEBI requires investors to either nominate or explicitly opt out of nomination for demat accounts and mutual fund folios. Accounts without either choice can face restrictions; adding a nominee is free.
