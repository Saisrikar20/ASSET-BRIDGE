# Planning ahead while alive
## Nominate everywhere
A nominee is the single biggest factor in how fast a family gets money. Every bank account, fixed deposit, EPF account, insurance policy, demat account and mutual fund folio allows a nominee. Banks now allow up to four nominees on deposit accounts. Updating a nominee is free.
## Keep names consistent
Name mismatches between PAN, Aadhaar and account records (for example initials vs full name) are a frequent cause of claim rejection. Update KYC so the name matches PAN everywhere.
## Write a will
A nominee holds money as a trustee; legal heirs under succession law or a will are the ultimate owners. A registered will reduces disputes and can avoid the need for a succession certificate in many cases, though probate may be required in some cities (Mumbai, Kolkata, Chennai for certain wills).
## Keep a record
Most unclaimed money exists because the family did not know the account existed. A consolidated map of every holding, shared with the nominees, prevents it.
