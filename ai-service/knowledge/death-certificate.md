# Death registration and certificates
## Civil Registration System
Under the Registration of Births and Deaths Act, 1969 (amended in 2023), every death must be reported to the local registrar within 21 days. Hospitals report institutional deaths; for deaths at home, the head of household reports. The registrar issues a death certificate, now linked to the national database under the Civil Registration System (CRS). Late registration after 21 days requires additional permission and, after a year, a magistrate's order.
## How many copies
Every bank, insurer, EPFO and fund house keeps a certified copy. Families should request several certified copies at once, or use the digitally signed certificate downloadable from CRS or the state portal.
## Medical certificate of cause of death
Insurers often also ask for the medical certificate of cause of death (MCCD) and, for accidental deaths, the FIR and post-mortem report.
